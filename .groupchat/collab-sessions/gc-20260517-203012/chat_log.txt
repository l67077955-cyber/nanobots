## CMHK管培生岗位 — AI Agent方向完全对口

### 核心发现：GT分两大stream

**Technical Stream（技术方向）← 你的目标**
- 专业要求：Computer Science, Communications Engineering, IT, Software Engineering, **Information Security, Artificial Intelligence** → **AI直接列在专业要求里**
- 职责明确包含AI：

> *"Leverage big data analytics and AI technologies to perform network performance monitoring, user behavior analysis... enhancing operational efficiency through technological empowerment."*
> 
> *"Drive the application and promotion of AI and cloud computing across diverse business scenarios."*

**Non-technical Stream** — 市场、HR、财务等，少量名额

### AI Agent在你面试中的定位策略

**面试官问"Why this role?"时：**
> "I noticed that the GT programme explicitly mentions leveraging AI and big data to enhance network operations and customer experience. My passion is AI Agent — autonomous systems that perceive, reason, and act. In telecom, this applies directly to: intelligent network O&M (agents auto-detecting and fixing anomalies), AI-driven customer service (agents handling complex queries), and network resource optimization. CMHK is uniquely positioned as Hong Kong's largest operator with the backing of China Mobile's 九天 AI platform — I want to bring my AI Agent expertise to drive that transformation."

**面试官问"Where do you see yourself in 3-5 years?"时：**
> "Year 1-2: Through rotations, I want to understand CMHK's network operations, customer touchpoints, and enterprise solutions from the ground up. Year 3-5: I aim to lead AI Agent implementation projects — building intelligent systems that automate network management and enhance customer experience. I see CMHK as the place where I can grow from a trainee into a technical leader shaping Hong Kong's smart city infrastructure."

| 场景 | CMHK具体应用 |
|---|---|
| **网络智能运维** | AI Agent自动检测网络异常+根因分析+自愈 → 5G网络运维 |

| **网络资源编排** | Agent动态分配频谱/带宽资源 |

1. **英文自我介绍里嵌入AI Agent关键词**（1-2句即可，展示方向性）

[Kirk]: ## 结论

**你面试的Graduate Trainee是通用管培生（商业方向），不是技术岗。CMHK另有一个TechNova Trainee Programme才是AI方向，但要求PhD/MPhil。**

## 关键发现

| 项目 | Graduate Trainee（你的） | TechNova Trainee |
|---|---|---|
| 定位 | 通用管培，商业管理 | 技术专才，研发创新 |
| 学历 | 本科+，任何学科 | **PhD/MPhil应届** |
| 方向 | Marketing/Finance/HR/Legal | Big Data/**AI**/Cybersecurity/Cloud/5G |
| 与AI Agent关联 | 间接 | 直接 |

**你的岗位要求：** 热衷ICT行业 + 本科毕业 + ≤1年工作经验，轮岗商业职能部门。

## 面试策略

- **把AI兴趣包装成"技术理解力+商业思维"组合优势**，不要说"我想做技术"
- 重点展示对电信AI应用的理解（AI客服、网络优化、智能营销）
- 职业规划说对**数字化转型/企业解决方案**感兴趣——既符合管培定位，又离AI最近
- **不要说这个岗位只是跳板**——commitment不足会扣分

## 备注

- 如果拿到offer，需权衡：管培生平台价值 vs AI方向偏离
- TechNova项目硬性要求PhD/MPhil，本科背景不符合门槛
- 长期看，管培生定岗到企业解决方案/产品部门后，仍可推动AI应用落地
