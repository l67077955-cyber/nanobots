## Interview Small Talk & Flow — English Daily Conversation

### 1. 到达接待处

**Receptionist:** "Good afternoon, how can I help you?"
**You:** "Good afternoon. I'm here for the 4:30 interview with the HR team. My name is [Your Name]."

**Receptionist:** "Please take a seat. Someone will be with you shortly."
**You:** "Thank you."

### 2. 面试官进门/打招呼

**Interviewer:** "Nice to meet you, Lu Jiaxiang. I'm Peter Chan from the HR team."
**You:** "Nice to meet you too, Mr. Chan. Thank you for having me today."

**Interviewer:** "How was your trip getting here?"
**You:** "Pretty smooth — Kwai Hing station is just a short walk from here. Very convenient."

**Interviewer:** "Would you like some water?"
**You:** "Yes, please. Thank you."

### 3. 面试结束/提问环节

**Interviewer:** "That's all the questions from us. Do you have any questions for us?"
**You:** "Yes, I have a few. Could you tell me more about how the rotation across different streams works in the GT programme?"

**Interviewer:** (answers)
**You:** "That sounds great. And my second question — where do you see MyLink's role evolving in CMHK's digital strategy over the next few years?"

### 4. 结束/道别

**Interviewer:** "We'll get back to you within two weeks."
**You:** "Thank you very much for your time today. I really enjoyed our conversation and learning more about CMHK."

**Interviewer:** "Nice meeting you."
**You:** "Nice meeting you too. Have a great day."

### 5. 突发情况

**没听清：**
- "Sorry, could you repeat that?"
- "I didn't quite catch that — could you say it again?"

**需要思考时间：**
- "That's a good question. Let me think for a moment."
- "Interesting question. Let me give you a concrete example..."

**面试官问要不要水/空调冷不冷：**
- "I'm fine, thank you."
- "Yes, a glass of water would be great."
