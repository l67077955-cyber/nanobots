General Questions

What is GeForce NOW?
GeForce NOW is NVIDIA’s cloud- game streaming service, delivering real-time RTX-powered gameplay straight from the cloud to your laptop, desktop, Mac, Chromebook, SHIELD TV, select Samsung and LG TVs, iPhone, iPad, Android devices, Steam Deck, VR headsets, Linux PC and more. Connect to your favorite game store accounts and stream games you own, or check out hundreds of favorite free-to-play games. With cloud saves for supported games, you can pick up your game where you left off, on any supported device, wherever you are.


Where is GeForce NOW available?
GeForce NOW is currently available from NVIDIA in North America, Europe, Japan, India and through GeForce NOW Alliance partners in other parts of the world. Visit the server status page for a list of available servers and their current status.


How do I join GeForce NOW, and how much does it cost?
Visit our membership page and choose the plan that best fits your gaming style. GeForce NOW offers three membership options including Performance and Ultimate with instant access to the best available GeForce gaming experience in the cloud. A Free membership is available for those looking to try the service. Day passes are also available for those looking to experience premium benefits, without a monthly commitment.


What games are available?
GeForce NOW supports two types of games to give you the widest choice of titles possible, with an expansive cloud gaming library of over 4,500 games.

Ready-to-Play games are available to stream instantly and always kept up to date. They’re tested for a seamless experience.

In addition, premium members have access to Install-to-Play games, which they can install, download and play thousands of additional Steam games that have opted-in to Steam Cloud Gaming by the game publisher, with more to be added each week. 

Learn more about Install-to-Play, or visit the GeForce NOW games page to see which games are currently available.


Do I need to own games?
Yes, you need to own or purchase a game from one of the supported game stores before playing it on GeForce NOW. The service also supports hundreds of the most popular free-to-play games. Members can play games they already own by connecting to top PC game stores, including Ubisoft Connect, Battle.net, EA app, GOG, Epic Games Store, Steam and Xbox.


Why are some games available in other countries but I cannot play them?
GeForce NOW follows local content-rating agencies. We try to have all supported games available in all countries, but some games are prohibited in some countries. Visit our games page to see which titles are available.


I’m having an issue while streaming a game. Where can I get help?
You can work with a Customer Care agent here. Our product and support teams are ready to assist when they can. You can alert us to your issue by pressing the exclamation icon in-app.


I have a feature request, where can I post it?
You can use the Send Feedback tool in the GeForce NOW app to share your feature requests and feedback. Our product and support teams read all feedback submitted, and your input helps drive new features and additions.


What are the minimum requirements, including internet speed, to play GeForce NOW?
Please visit the System Requirements page for info on system requirements, including internet speed.


I created an NVIDIA account, but I can't play.
After creating an NVIDIA account, you may need to take an additional step of selecting a membership type. You can do this either by visiting geforcenow.com and clicking “Join Now”, or by visiting the NVIDIA account portal to select a membership.

How much monthly playtime do I get per month?
Performance and Ultimate members get 100 hours of monthly playtime. Up to 15 hours of unused playtime can be rolled over to the next month.


What happens if I run out of monthly playtime?
Playtime resets every month. You can check your exact reset date anytime in your Account Portal. If you reach your max playtime within the month, you can purchase additional playtime hours to keep playing with all your premium benefits. Additional playtime can be purchased directly within your Account Portal. Or continue playing with access to basic rigs and no access to other premium member benefits until your playtime resets. Why did you introduce a monthly playtime limit?
We rolled out a 100-hour monthly playtime limit to continue providing exceptional quality and speed — as well as shorter queue times — for Performance and Ultimate members. 

If members reach the max playtime during a billing month, they can purchase additional premium playtime or continue playing as with a free user experience until playtime is reset on the next billing date.


I am grandfathered and have unlimited playtime. What’s next?
If you are grandfathered into unlimited playtime from a subscription purchase prior to January 15, 2026, unlimited playtime will continue until the first billing date after January 15, 2026. At which point memberships come with 100 hours of playtime, with up to 15 hours of rollover, per month, and the ability to purchase additional hours.

Install-to-Play

What is Install-to-Play and how does it work?
The new Install-to-Play feature enables premium members to download and install over thousands of Steam games opted-in for Steam Cloud Gaming directly onto GeForce NOW cloud storage hard drives, similar to a local gaming PC experience. More will be added each week.

100GB of single session cloud storage will be included with both Ultimate and Performance memberships. Members who want persistent cloud storage to save all game installs and game saves across sessions (for games that don’t support Steam cloud saves) will have the flexibility to add-on additional cloud storage on top of their memberships. You can sign up for a premium membership here.


How do I know which games on GeForce NOW are Install-to-Play?
You can browse all the Install-to-Play games in the GeForce NOW app by going to the Install-to-Play section and clicking SEE ALL, checking game details for the Install-to-Play label, or by using the filters in the app to see all Install-to-Play games.


What is the difference between Install-to-Play and Ready-to-Play games?
Install-to-Play games are titles you can install and play from your own Steam library, as long as the publisher has opted in to support Steam Cloud Gaming. You manage what you play, and can optionally purchase a storage plan to keep your installed games and saved data in the cloud for games that don’t support Steam cloud saves. More will be added as publishers opt-in their games for Steam Cloud Gaming.

Ready-to-Play games are designed for instant access, and are highlighted each week on GFN Thursday. They are always kept updated, tested for a seamless experience, and NVIDIA manages all the backend work, ensuring the most popular games are readily available. You can jump right into playing them without any installation steps. Ready-to-Play games do not count against your 100 GB storage.


Do Install-to-Play game downloads and game progress automatically save?
No. Persistent cloud storage is required to keep your game installed and game save progress for games that don’t support Steam cloud saves. Learn more about storage requirements.
---
[用户]: What are the different types of cloud storage for Install-to-Play games?
GeForce NOW offers two types of cloud storage for Install-to-Play games:

Single-Session Cloud Storage: This type of storage requires the game to be downloaded at the start of each playing session. 100GB of single-session storage is included with Performance and Ultimate memberships. Some games may require more storage to start the install, or you might still want persistent storage to save your game installation for future gaming. NOTE: Games that support Steam Cloud saves will keep your game save progress for future sessions.
Persistent Cloud Storage: Available as an optional add-on purchase, this storage option lets you save your downloaded games after the first install and your saved game data - if the game does not support Steam Cloud saves - for future sessions. 200GB, 500GB, and 1TB options are available for purchase.

How much extra storage can I buy?
All premium members will get 100GB of single-session cloud storage included with their membership. They also have the flexibility to add on larger cloud storage options — 200GB will be available for $2.99/month, 500 GB for $4.99/month, or 1 TB for $7.99/month.

Note: Storage add-ons are not currently available to SEPA users. For more information about SEPA limitations, please see this knowledgebase article.


I am a free user, can I pay for persistent storage?
No. Install-to-Play games are available for premium members only. You need to upgrade to a premium membership first, and then purchase persistent storage. 


If I’m in a data center in one region and install games with permanent storage, then switch to a data center in another region, will that affect the games I play on Install-to-Play?
When you first purchase an Install-to-Play game, your persistent storage location is set. You can change this later if needed, but keep in mind — changing it will erase all of your saved data for Install-to-Play games.

Also, note that for Install-to-Play titles, the server location setting in the GeForce NOW app won’t apply; these games always run from the storage location you selected. The server location preference still works normally for Ready-to-Play games.


Is Install-to-Play currently only available for steam games?
Yes.

### 1. 降级到免费 = 事实上的取消
> "Any accrued account balance is forfeited upon downgrading to a free account."
> （降级到免费账户后，账户余额将被清零）

### 2. 管理入口

> （活跃订阅绑定的付款方式无法删除，需先取消订阅）

---

### 实际操作步骤（FAQ 隐含的流程）

1. 打开 GeForce NOW 客户端 → **Settings** → **GeForce NOW Membership**
2. 点击 **Manage** → 进入网页选择 **Free** 计划（降级 = 取消）
3. 降级后回到 App 删除付款方式

> ⚠️ 降级后当前计费周期仍可使用，余额不退。

---

**结论**：NVIDIA 的 FAQ 刻意回避了"cancel"这个词，用"downgrade to free"替代。操作路径是 **Settings → Membership → Manage → 选 Free**。
