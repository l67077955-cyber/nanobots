## 一、必考题（几乎100%会问）

### 1. Tell me about yourself / 自我介绍

**答题框架（1-2分钟）：**
> I'm [name], a [major] graduate from [university]. During my studies, I [1-2 key experiences: internship/project/leadership]. What excites me about CMHK is [specific reason]. I'm looking forward to contributing my [skill] to [specific area].

**示例：**
> I'm a Business Analytics graduate from XX University. During my internship at XX, I led a data-driven project that improved customer retention by 15%. What draws me to CMHK is the company's transformation from a traditional telco to a digital services provider — especially the HKBN integration creating a converged mobile-fixed ecosystem. I believe my analytical skills and customer-centric mindset can contribute to CMHK's enterprise solutions team.

### 2. Why CMHK? / 为什么选择中国移动香港？

**答题要点（3选2展开）：**
- **行业地位**：香港最大移动运营商，800万+用户，5G覆盖率领先
- **转型势头**：收购HKBN（63亿港元），从纯移动→移动+固网+企业解决方案
- **大湾区机遇**：背靠中国移动集团，连接内地与香港的桥梁角色
- **数字化转型**：5G-A、AI网络优化、智慧城市项目

**示例：**
> Three reasons. First, CMHK is Hong Kong's largest mobile operator with over 8 million subscribers, giving graduates unparalleled exposure to scale. Second, the HKBN acquisition signals a strategic shift toward converged services — that's where the industry is heading, and I want to be part of that transformation. Third, as a subsidiary of China Mobile, CMHK has unique advantages in the Greater Bay Area integration, which I find exciting both professionally and personally.

### 3. Why telecom? / 为什么选择电信行业？

**答题要点：**
- 电信是数字经济的**基础设施**，所有行业都依赖
- 5G/AI/IoT正在重塑行业，不是传统夕阳产业
- 与用户生活高度相关，影响面大

**示例：**
> Telecom is the backbone of the digital economy. Every industry — finance, healthcare, logistics — runs on connectivity. With 5G-A, AI-driven network optimization, and IoT scaling up, this industry is undergoing its most significant transformation since the smartphone era. I want to build my career at the intersection of technology and people, and telecom is exactly that intersection.

### 4. Where do you see yourself in 3-5 years? / 职业规划

**答题框架：**
> Year 1-2: 深入学习业务，轮岗积累 → Year 3-5: 成为某领域专家，带领小项目 → 长期：管理岗/战略岗

**示例：**
> In the first two years, I want to build a solid foundation across CMHK's core business areas through the rotation programme — understanding everything from network operations to customer experience. By year 3-5, I aim to specialize in [enterprise solutions/digital transformation/product strategy] and lead cross-functional projects. Long-term, I see myself in a strategic leadership role, helping CMHK capture opportunities in the Greater Bay Area market.

### 5. What's your biggest weakness? / 最大缺点

**答题原则：** 说真实的、但可控的缺点 + 正在改进的具体行动

**示例：**
> I tend to over-analyze before making decisions, which can slow things down in fast-paced situations. I've been working on this by setting personal deadlines for analysis phases and using the 80/20 rule — making decisions when I have 80% of the information rather than waiting for 100%.

## 二、行为题（STAR法，准备3-4个故事覆盖以下主题）

### 6. Tell me about a time you demonstrated leadership.

**STAR模板：**
- **S**: 在XX项目中，团队5人，deadline紧，意见分歧
- **T**: 需要在2周内完成方案，但团队方向不统一
- **A**: 我组织了一次brainstorming，让每人提方案→投票→整合最优方案；同时主动承担数据分析部分
- **R**: 按时交付，获得导师/客户好评，团队关系更紧密

### 7. Describe a time you handled conflict with a teammate.

**答题要点：** 不要说"我赢了"，要说"我理解了对方→找到共同目标→达成共识"

**示例：**
> During a group project, a teammate and I disagreed on the research methodology. Instead of arguing, I proposed we each run a small pilot with our respective approaches and compare results. His approach turned out better for qualitative insights, mine for quantitative. We combined both, and our final report received the highest grade. This taught me that conflicts often stem from different valid perspectives.

### 8. Tell me about a time you failed. What did you learn?

**答题要点：** 选一个真实的、不太致命的失败 + 具体的反思和改变

**示例：**
> In my second year, I led a campus event that only attracted 30% of expected attendance. I realized I had focused too much on logistics and not enough on promotion. I learned that execution matters, but so does marketing and understanding your audience. For the next event, I created a promotional timeline with weekly milestones, and attendance tripled.

### 9. Give me an example of when you went above and beyond.

**答题要点：** 超出职责范围，主动发现问题并解决

## 三、行业知识题（可能问1-2个）

### 10. What do you know about CMHK's recent developments?

**必背要点：**
- **HKBN收购**（2025年）：63亿港元收购HKBN 71%股权，整合移动+固网宽带，成为香港唯一同时拥有大规模移动和固网资产的运营商
- **5G-A部署**：推进5G-Advanced网络升级
- **企业解决方案**：面向企业的云、网络安全、物联网服务
- **ESG承诺**：碳中和目标、数字包容

### 11. How do you see AI impacting the telecom industry?

**答题要点：**
- **网络优化**：AI预测流量高峰，自动调整网络资源
- **客户服务**：智能客服、个性化推荐
- **新收入来源**：AI-as-a-Service，为企业客户提供AI基础设施
- **运营效率**：自动化运维，降低成本

### 12. What are the biggest challenges facing Hong Kong's telecom industry?

**答题要点：**
- **市场饱和**：渗透率超200%，增长靠ARPU提升而非用户增长
- **价格竞争**：低价套餐压缩利润
- **监管环境**：频谱分配、消费者保护法规
- **技术投资**：5G/6G、AI基础设施需要持续投入
- **人才竞争**：与金融、科技行业争夺人才
