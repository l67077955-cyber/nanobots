## 一、自我介绍骨架（英文45秒）

```
I'm Lu Jiaxiang — CS master at HKU, software engineering at Zhejiang University.

Three things define me:

1. **Telco experience** — At Zhejiang Mobile, I processed terabytes of subscriber data and built anti-fraud models on Hive/Spark. I know how a telecom network generates data and how to extract value from it.

2. **AI research** — My KDD 2026 paper (under review) focuses on neural-collapse prompt tuning for cross-modal alignment. That's AI + Edge Computing in 5G context: smaller models, faster inference, real-time decisions.

3. **Cross-border DNA** — ZJU trained me on mainland standards. HKU trained me on global standards. I bridge the gap — exactly what CMHK's GBA strategy needs.

Why CMHK? Because I've already done telecom data at scale. I want to turn that into product impact at Hong Kong's largest operator.
```

## 三、高频面试问题 — 英文回答框架

### Q1: Why CMHK, not other operators?
```
"Three reasons:
1. Scale — 8M+ subscribers, market leader. I want to solve problems that matter.
2. GBA position — No other operator has CMHK's cross-border integration. The GBA expansion is a once-in-a-decade opportunity.
3. Tech trajectory — 5G-A + AICDE is the right roadmap. AI-native networks are where the industry is going, and CMHK is investing seriously in it."
```

### Q2: What's your biggest strength?
```
"Bridging research and engineering. My KDD paper is theoretical — but at Zhejiang Mobile, I took similar techniques and deployed them in production. I don't just publish papers; I ship code."
```

### Q3: What's your biggest weakness?
```
"Early in my career, I dominated discussions instead of facilitating them. A WeChat message from my classmates made me realize: 'What I thought was pushing for excellence, they experienced as making everyone miserable.' I've been actively working on listening first, speaking second — count to three before responding."
```

### Q4: Tell me about a failure.
```
"My first research project — I spent 3 months building a complex model nobody used. I assumed technical sophistication = value. When the team chose a simpler baseline, I was frustrated. I learned: understand the user before writing a single line of code. Now I always start with 'why does this matter?' before 'how do I build it?'"
```

### Q5: Where do you see yourself in 3 years?
```
"Graduating from the GT programme with a 360-degree view of how a telecom operator actually runs. I want to be the person who connects technical teams with business strategy — translating network capabilities into revenue opportunities, especially around GBA cross-border data services."
```

## 五、金句卡（选2句背熟）

1. **"A great model with no business understanding is just a published paper. A good model with deep business understanding is a product."**
2. **"Technical people optimize for metrics; business people optimize for risk. I need to speak both languages."**
3. **"Observe first, ask indirectly, earn the right to direct feedback."**（文化适应）
4. **"Cloud handles heavy training; Edge handles real-time inference. They're complementary."**（AICDE的CE）
5. **"I'm not here to be the smartest person in the room. I'm here to make the whole team smarter."**（团队合作）
